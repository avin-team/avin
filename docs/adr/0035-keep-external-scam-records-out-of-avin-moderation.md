# Keep external scam records out of Avin moderation

CheckScam, ChongScam, and similar services may inform product research, but their records will not be imported as Avin-native Public Risk Reports or included in Avin statistics because Avin cannot inherit their reporter attribution, evidence chain, consent, correction history, or moderation finding. Any future external lookup must remain a clearly provenance-labelled source with separate storage and public presentation; only a report submitted and moderated under Avin's own policy may be presented as an Avin Public Warning.
