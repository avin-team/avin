# Match Provider Bond before Provider review

Provider applicants choose a Bond amount of at least 1,000,000 VND and receive a 24-hour dedicated VietQR instruction whose exact SePay match gates entry into application review; this payment intent and its custody account remain separate from UserWallet deposits and marketplace ledgers. Matching binds the application and derived financial presentation to the policy version snapshotted by the intent, locks that Bond amount until review ends, and approval atomically confirms that the Bond has not entered refund before creating the active standing and first public version. The exact recognized Bond is public, while versioned policy thresholds derive Provider Tier and Recommended Transaction Limit automatically; applicants must explicitly acknowledge publication of that amount and their full registered account numbers. Late or mismatched transfers, refunds, and manual adjustments are audited `SUPER_ADMIN` actions without dual approval. P0 collects no Membership Fee, allows a 2FA-authorized Provider to top up through a new 24-hour intent after approval, and permits full exit but no voluntary partial Bond withdrawal. This supersedes ADR-0029's dual-control requirement for Bond operations and ADR-0031's manual-only reconciliation model, while retaining ADR-0031's dedicated legal-entity custody account and prohibition on using personal or Avin wallet accounts.
