# Require a complete flow-specific evidence bundle before Risk Report submission

A Risk Report may remain an auto-saved draft with incomplete evidence, but **Gửi để Avin duyệt** is enabled only after the selected flow's required evidence bundle is complete, every retained file has passed scanning, at least one safe material Public Evidence Copy exists, and the reporter has previewed the exact public packet. Transaction, fake-surface, and account-reclaim flows each enforce their own structural bundle before submission; `CHANGES_REQUESTED` remains available for evidentiary quality, ambiguity, or linkage problems that require human judgment rather than for obviously missing file kinds. Allowing one-file submissions would reduce initial friction but conflict with Avin's high-verification positioning and waste limited moderator capacity on predictably incomplete reports.
