# Make Provider verification identity-bound and non-transferable

Every Protection Provider will pass a fresh evidence-backed review, and neither a profile, tenure, directory position, badge, nor verification slot may be sold or transferred to another identity. Applications remain open year-round with explicit approve, change-request, or reject outcomes rather than scarce scheduled slots or silent rejection. This prevents verified reputation from becoming a tradable asset whose history no longer describes the person or business shown on the profile.
