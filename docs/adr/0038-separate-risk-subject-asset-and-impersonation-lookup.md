# Separate accused-subject, asset-history, and impersonation lookup

A Risk Report may mention accused counterparties, payment destinations, intermediaries, contact channels, listings, reclaimed digital assets, and identities being impersonated, but appearance in a report does not itself make an identifier risky. Exact public lookup therefore exposes three explicit result classes after whole-report approval: a Risk Subject Identifier produces a role-labelled Risk Warning for an accused or operational identifier; a Risk Asset Identifier produces a neutral history alert for an account or asset reported as reclaimed; and an Impersonated Identifier produces a neutral alert that directs users to the fake locators. A payment destination can enter the first class when evidence proves its role in the incident even when legal ownership is unknown, but the page says only that it received funds and does not call its account holder a scammer. Only the first class contributes to risk-warning totals, while asset and genuine impersonated identities never inherit accused standing. P0 groups by exact normalized namespace and value, never by name or fuzzy person-level entity resolution; one report may cross-link several result pages without being duplicated. This adds schema and UI discipline but lets MMO users check payment destinations and reclaimed UIDs while preventing unsupported ownership claims or false labels against victims and genuine Providers.
