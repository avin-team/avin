# Keep external transaction protection separate from marketplace commerce

Avin will model protection and public risk reporting for transactions occurring outside the marketplace as their own domain; they will not reuse marketplace `Seller`, `Order`, `Dispute`, or `EscrowHold` semantics. A `Provider Bond` is transferred and managed directly by an Admin outside Avin's automated payment flows, while Avin records its recognized amount and immutable manual adjustments for the public Protection Provider profile; any support is coordinated and paid through direct external contact rather than an in-app claim, ticket, chat, or payout workflow. Public Risk Reports may concern registered Providers or unrelated external subjects, and cannot themselves mutate marketplace orders, wallets, escrow, ledger balances, or Provider Bonds. P0 covers direct Protection Providers only: no GDV, sub-agent network, delegated verification, subordinate collateral, or Provider-operated trust directory inherits Avin's verified standing.
