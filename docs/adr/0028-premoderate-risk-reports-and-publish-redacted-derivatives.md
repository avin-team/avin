# Premoderate Risk Reports and publish only redacted evidence derivatives

Every Public Risk Report will require a verified private reporter contact and sufficient type-specific evidence, pass Admin moderation before publication, and retain append-only correction/removal history. Submitted evidence remains immutable and private; only an Admin-created derivative with unrelated personal data redacted, metadata removed, and an Avin watermark may appear publicly. Exact normalized identifiers group report history, but sensitive values remain masked in every public projection, including HTML metadata and analytics. This trades immediate crowdsourced publication for lower defamation, doxxing, evidence-leak, and irreversible cache/index exposure.

P0 will not include public comments. Additional evidence enters through a new or supplemental moderated report, and only currently published reports contribute to public statistics.
