# Listing archival retention

Listings that have entered the public lifecycle (`PUBLISHED`, `PAUSED`, `HIDDEN`, or `ARCHIVED`) are never hard-deleted in P0. Archival is terminal and preserves those Listings, their moderation audit, and historical media references so Order history and platform accountability remain intact. A Seller may hard-delete an unpublished `DRAFT` Listing because it has never been available to Buyers and cannot have Order history.
