# Separate Risk Reporting Restriction from Seller Enforcement

Deliberately fabricated evidence, brigading, extortion, or repeated abuse may cause one Risk Moderator to place a 30-day Risk Reporting Restriction on the responsible account with a mandatory reason. It blocks new or revised Risk Reports while preserving login, history, responses, and correction access; the account may request one review, and a later violation may receive another 30-day restriction without introducing a P0 duration ladder. This restriction never automatically changes Seller Enforcement, Protection Provider standing, or Bond; conduct that also violates marketplace or Provider policy requires a separate authorized decision in that domain. A shared ban would be simpler but would wrongly make abuse of a community-reporting capability equivalent to a commercial Seller violation.
