# Preserve independent reports under aggregate Risk Warnings

Each approved Risk Report produces one globally owned Published Risk Report with the reporter's redacted narrative and approved evidence, while a Risk Warning groups related published reports for identifier lookup. A report containing several Risk Subject Identifiers may appear from several Risk Warning pages without being copied into separate reports. Avin never rewrites several testimonies into one summary or transfers ownership between reporters; retries and same-incident duplicates do not increase the public report count, but independently evidenced incidents remain visible separately. This trades a simpler single-summary page for authentic testimony, correction history, and provenance that the MMO community can assess.
